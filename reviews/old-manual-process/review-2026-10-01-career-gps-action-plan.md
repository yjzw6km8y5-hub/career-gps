# Review — Career GPS Action Plan

- ERROR — `“six journeys fully deep”` plus two countries, bilingual UX, calculators and reports in Weeks 2–5 is not realistic for a part-time non-engineer. Make cardiologist and musician the two complete demonstrators; keep four route shells, then deepen only after testing.
- ERROR — `Home: “Pick any future.”` omits the three entry modes discussed: “I know the career,” “I know my interests,” and “I don’t know yet.” Add interest/activity exploration without claiming an unvalidated psychometric assessment.
- ERROR — `“State choice rule”` does not define the first customer or operating scope. Record India Grades 8–12 (or explicitly change it), one board/state/language, and Ontario Grades 9–12 newcomer families, English first.
- ERROR — `“Looks like the full vision”` omits the common engine with separate state/province data modules. Specify profile, career graph, academic rules, costs, aid, timeline/reminders, alternatives, and progress/course-correction modules.
- ERROR — `“honest realism funnel”` is underspecified. Show opportunity/base-rate evidence, readiness indicators, controllable milestones and income volatility; never fabricate personal success probabilities or guarantee admissions, scholarships or careers.
- ERROR — `“Regional language + English”` misses the accessibility requirements: mobile-first/PWA, low bandwidth, voice/audio, minimal typing, large visual cards, WhatsApp/print support and teacher-assisted use.
- ERROR — `“parent-controlled consented accounts”` omits a portable student-controlled Career Passport, school access only by permission, data minimization, no Aadhaar/bank credentials, and source/version/expiry/update-owner fields.
- ERROR — `“Buyers: [3] CSR/foundation funders and [3] private schools”` misses the requested model tests: free basic/paid roadmap, family subscription, counsellor consult, school licence, CSR/government sponsorship, bank/RESP partnerships and disclosed non-steering referrals. Add pricing and preorder experiments.
- ERROR — The plan omits explicit requested deliverables: India/Ontario competitor comparison, 20 interview questions, ten-career schema/backlog, screen-by-screen prototype, realistic cost estimate, legal/accuracy risks, and measurable success, pivot and shutdown thresholds. Add these before the pilot; use placeholders where decisions are still missing.

- UNCERTAIN — The Karnataka professors may not validate younger rural users, regional-language UX, arts/sports routes or provide school access. Confirm this and add a school teacher champion, pathway reviewer and Kannada reviewer.
